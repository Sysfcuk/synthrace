# M2 review: "Speed and Feel"

**Status:** implemented, tested natively and in the browser (WebGPU and WebGL2), and deployed.

**Not judged yet:** handling and audio feel need a human playtest. The agent could only drive in a throttled, hidden browser pane, and cannot hear audio.

## What was built

### Simulation (`sim`)

**Traffic** (`traffic.rs`):
- Four archetypes: coupe, van, truck and sports car, each with its own speed range, size and lane-change habit.
- Cars follow their lane and match the speed of a slower car ahead.
- Lane changes are telegraphed: 1 s of indicator, then a 1.6 s move. Cars never start a lane change within 160 m ahead of the player.
- **Fair spawning:**
  - Rows of 1–3 cars spawn 900 m ahead.
  - Every row leaves at least one lane free.
  - No spawn ever lands inside the 1.2 s reaction window.
- **Anti-wall rule:** a car slows down rather than complete a four-abreast row.

**Collisions:**
- **Traffic:**
  - A sideswipe pushes the cars apart.
  - A rear-end at less than 40 km/h of speed difference is a bump: −30% speed and a wobble.
  - A harder rear-end is a 1.7 s spin-out.
  - Being rear-ended while slow gives a small shove.
- **Palms:**
  - Glancing contact scrapes off 15% of speed.
  - A square hit is a 2.3 s crash.

**Near misses and overtakes:** detected when the player's centre passes a car's centre.
- A near miss needs a gap under 0.35 car-widths and a speed difference over 30 km/h. A gap under 0.15 car-widths counts as "CLOSE!".
- A car you've touched never pays out.

**Drift:**
- Enter by holding drift with at least 30% steer above 120 km/h, or by tapping the brake while steering.
- Centrifugal push is reduced to 0.45× while drifting, and there's a 0.1 g speed drag.
- Charge tiers arrive at 0.8, 1.8 and 3.0 s. Charging runs twice as fast when the drift angle is tight.
- Releasing gives a boost of +8%, +12% or +15% (by tier), plus nitro.

**Nitro:**
- Meter from 0 to 100; you start at 34.
- Drains at 25 per second.
- Raises top speed by 20% and acceleration by 25%.
- Earned from near misses (+6, or +10 for a close one), overtakes (+2) and drifting. Earning runs at 1.5× while nitro is burning.

**Gears:** six automatic gears, with an RPM model and a 120 ms torque cut on each upshift.

**Events:** a typed `SimEvent` stream (near miss, overtake, bump, spin-out, crash, scrape, drift start/tier/boost, nitro on/off, gear shift).

**Autopilot:** for attract mode. It picks lanes by time-to-collision, never crosses a car alongside, and brakes for anything in its own path.

### Content and rendering

- **Procedural traffic meshes** for all four archetypes (coupe, van, truck with neon trailer stripes, sports car). Their dimensions are checked against the `sim` collision boxes by a test.
- **Per-instance lights:** brake lights and blinking amber indicators, driven from the simulation state through emissive marker codes.
- **Renderer split into modules** (`gpu.rs`, `post.rs`, `lib.rs`), with batched instancing for six mesh types.
- **Additive HDR particle pass** with round sprites and streaks, bent with the world.
- **Speed effects:**
  - stronger radial blur and chromatic aberration with nitro;
  - FOV kick of +12° on nitro and +5–9° on drift boosts;
  - camera shake that scales with impact (trauma²);
  - an impact or nitro screen flash;
  - hit-stop of 35–80 ms on crashes and close calls.
- **CPU particles** (`game::vfx`):
  - drift sparks in tier colours (orange, cyan, magenta, white);
  - tyre smoke and twin-exhaust nitro flames;
  - off-road dust and impact sparks;
  - near-miss streak flurries;
  - world-anchored speed lines.

### Audio (all synthesised)

- **Engine:** the firing frequency follows RPM. Two detuned saws plus a sub, through a filter driven by throttle and RPM, with saturation, off-throttle crackle and a dip plus pop on gear shifts.
- **Tyres:** squeal made from band-passed noise plus a wobbling tone, scaled by slip.
- **Nitro:** a hiss while burning, and an ignition roar when it starts.
- **Traffic:** four pass-by voices for the nearest cars, each with Doppler, stereo pan and distance gain.
- **One-shots:** impact (thump plus crunch), near-miss whoosh panned to the passing side, and **key-locked near-miss blips** that climb with your streak. They're picked from the current chord, so they always fit the music.
- The music bus was lowered to 0.6 to make room for the effects.

### Input and HUD

- **Keyboard:** arrows/WASD to drive, Space/Shift to drift, N/Ctrl/X for nitro, M to mute, F3 for the debug overlay.
- **Gamepad** (standard mapping):
  - left stick or D-pad to steer, with a deadzone and response curve;
  - RT throttle, LT brake;
  - A or RB to drift, X or LB for nitro;
  - rumble on impacts and near misses.
- **Touch:**
  - holding a finger on the road steers by its position;
  - the car accelerates automatically;
  - BRAKE, DRIFT and NITRO buttons appear when the screen is first touched;
  - a "rotate for landscape" hint appears in portrait.
- **Minimal DOM HUD:** speed, gear, a 3-segment nitro bar (glows while burning), and animated callouts (CLOSE!, NEAR MISS, DRIFT I–III, BOOST, NITRO, CRASH, SPIN OUT). The full in-engine HUD arrives in M3.

## Determinism

- The golden hash is now `0xee1c339893585c25` and includes the state of all traffic.
- The wasm build reproduces it exactly, checked in the browser (and by the e2e suite on every deploy).

## Known weaknesses

- **Feel is unvalidated by play.** Drift entry, nitro strength, traffic density (row gap 75 m) and collision severity all need playtesting.
- **All traffic drives the same direction.** Oncoming lanes and the oncoming-lane bonus are planned for the two-way stages in M3/M4.
- **Hit-stop freezes the simulation, but the camera and particles keep animating.** That's intentional, but worth reviewing.
- **Additive smoke reads as glow,** not as opaque smoke.
- **No scoring or multiplier yet** (M3). Near misses currently feed only nitro and the callouts.
- **The DOM HUD is a stopgap** and doesn't bloom.
- **The traffic voice model is simple** (a saw plus a sub). It may sound buzzy at close range.
- **The car mesh has no visible brake lights** for the player's own braking; only traffic has them.
